ENotes beside Samsung Notes and Google Keep

Samsung Notes and Google Keep are good notes apps. ENotes is built for one thing they are not: notes that are encrypted with your own password and never leave the phone. This page sets them side by side, using only what Samsung and Google say about their own apps.

What ENotes does not do

ENotes does not sync between devices, has no web or computer version, and cannot share a note for others to edit with you. It does not turn handwriting into typed text or read text out of photos. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site, and because it cannot go online it cannot update itself.

Where the others do more

Samsung Notes syncs across Galaxy phones, tablets and computers through Samsung Cloud, and is built into Galaxy phones. Google Keep syncs across devices and the web and lets you share notes with other people. Both are made by large companies and come ready installed on many phones.
