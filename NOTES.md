ENotes 1.0, released 22 November 2026.

- Every note, title, tag, folder name, attachment, version and search history encrypted on the phone with AES-256-GCM.
- The main key is locked with your password or PIN (Argon2id, 64 MiB) and again with a key in the phone's security chip (StrongBox where the phone has one, otherwise its secure hardware).
- A 24-character recovery code shown at setup, and a way to make a new one.
- Fingerprint unlock, with the password needed after a restart and every 72 hours.
- Growing waits after four wrong tries, and an optional wipe after ten.
- Locking when the screen turns off and after a chosen time away. A separate lock for chosen notes and folders.
- Rich text: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight, colours, sizes, headings, quotes, bullets, numbers and checklists, with undo and redo.
- Photos, an in-app camera, a document scanner that saves PDFs, drawings with S Pen pressure and a laser pointer, voice recordings, PDFs you can write on, and any other file.
- Links between notes, templates, version history, reminders, a home-screen widget and a quick-panel tile.
- Folders, colours, tags, pins, favourites, smart folders, search and a 30-day recycle bin.
- Sharing as a password-protected .enote file, PDF, Markdown or text, and printing. Importing from Google Keep, text, Markdown and .enote files.
- An encrypted backup file and restore, with nothing included in the phone's own backup.
- A security check, a page explaining how your notes are protected, and Help.
- Light, dark and black themes, eight accent colours, four note fonts, and a choice of plainer names and icons.
- Screenshots blocked, a private keyboard, copied text cleared after 30 seconds, and no internet permission.
